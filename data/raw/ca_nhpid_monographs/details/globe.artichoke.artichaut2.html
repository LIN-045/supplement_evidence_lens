Globe Artichoke - Cynara Cardunculus

(PDF Version - 31 KB)

This monograph is intended to serve as a guide to industry for the preparation of Product Licence Applications (PLAs) and labels for natural health product market authorization. It is not intended to be a comprehensive review of the medicinal ingredient.

Notes

  • Text in parentheses is additional optional information which can be included on the label at the applicant's discretion.
  • The solidus (/) indicates that either term and/or statement may be selected on the label.

Date

January 30, 2026

References: Proper name: USDA 2024; Common names: Gardner and McGuffin 2013; Source information: EMA 2018; Bradley 2006; ESCOP 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000.

Route of Administration

Oral

Dosage Form(s)

This monograph excludes foods or food-like dosage forms as indicated in the Compendium of Monographs Guidance Document.

Acceptable dosage forms for oral use are indicated in the dosage form drop-down list of the web-based Product Licence Application form for Compendial applications.

Use(s) or Purpose(s)

  • Used in Herbal Medicine to help relieve digestive disturbances/dyspepsia (EMA 2018; Bradley 2006; ESCOP 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000).
  • Used in Herbal Medicine (as a choleretic) to help increase bile flow (Bradley 2006; Mills and Bone 2005; ESCOP 2003; Hoffmann 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000).
  • Source of antioxidants/Provides antioxidants (Olas 2024; Skarpanska-Stejnborn et al. 2008).
  • Source of antioxidants/Provides antioxidants that help fight/protect (cell) against/reduce (the oxidative effect of/the oxidative damage caused by/cell damage caused by) free radicals (Olas 2024; Skarpanska-Stejnborn et al. 2008).

Note: The above uses can be combined on the product label if from the same system of medicine (e.g., Used in Herbal Medicine to help relieve digestive disturbances and to help increase bile flow).

Dose(s)

Subpopulation(s)

Adults 18 years and older

Quantity(ies)

Dried leaf

Methods of preparation: Powdered, Non-Standardized Ethanolic Extracts (Dry extract, Tincture, Fluid extract)

Antioxidant

Not to exceed 10 grams of dried leaf, per day (EMA 2018; Mills and Bone 2005; ESCOP 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000).

Digestive disturbance/dyspepsia/Help increase bile flow

0.6 - 10 grams of dried leaf, per day (EMA 2018; Mills and Bone 2005; ESCOP 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000).

Method of preparation: Soft extract concentrate*

Antioxidant

Not to exceed 2.1 grams of soft extract concentrate, per day; equivalent to a maximum of 7.35 grams of dried leaf, per day; and do not exceed 0.7 grams of soft extract concentrate, per single dose (EMA 2018).

Digestive disturbance/dyspepsia/Help increase bile flow

0.7 grams of soft extract concentrate, 3 times per day; equivalent to 5.25 - 7.35 grams of dried leaf, per day (EMA 2018).

*Note: solvent: ethanol 20% (V/V) in water (EMA 2018).

Methods of preparation: Dry*, Non-Standardized Aqueous Extracts (Dry extract, Decoction, Decoction concentrate, Infusion, Infusion concentrate)

Antioxidant

Not to exceed 10 grams of dried leaf, per day (EMA 2018; Bradley 2006; Mills and Bone 2005; ESCOP 2003).

Digestive disturbance/dyspepsia/Help increase bile flow

1.5 - 10 grams of dried leaf, per day (EMA 2018; Bradley 2006; Mills and Bone 2005; ESCOP 2003).

*Note: Dried leaves should be prepared as an infusion or decoction by consumers prior to use (see direction for use).

Fresh leaf

Method of preparation: Dry extract*

Antioxidant

Not to exceed 2.7 grams of dry extract, per day; equivalent to a maximum of 94.5 grams of fresh leaf, per day; and not to exceed 900 milligrams of dry extract, per single dose (EMA 2018).

Digestive disturbance/dyspepsia/Help increase bile flow

200 - 900 milligrams of dry extract, 3 times per day; equivalent to 9 - 94.5 grams of fresh leaf, per day (EMA 2018).

*Note: solvent: water (EMA 2018).

Method of preparation: Soft extract concentrate*

Antioxidant

Not to exceed 1.8 grams of soft extract concentrate; equivalent to a maximum of 54 grams of fresh leaf, per day; and not to exceed 600 milligrams of soft extract concentrate, per single dose (EMA 2018).

Digestive disturbance/dyspepsia/Help increase bile flow

600 milligrams of soft extract concentrate, 3 times per day; equivalent to 27 - 54 grams of fresh leaf, per day (EMA 2018).

*Note: solvent: water (EMA 2018).

Direction(s) for use

Loose dosage form (for infusion/decoction)*

Place [insert volume to be measured by consumer, e.g., X teaspoons] of dried leaves in [insert the volume of water, e.g., ½ cup (125 mL)] of boiling/cold water. Infuse for [e.g., 5-15 minutes]/Bring to a boil and let simmer for [e.g., 10-20 minutes]. Strain and drink.

*Note: This direction of use statement can be modified on the label provided that it gives clear instructions to consumers.

Duration(s) of Use

No statement required.

Risk Information

Caution(s) and warning(s)

All products

  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you are pregnant (Barnes et al. 2007; Mills and Bone 2005; ESCOP 2003).
  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you have gallstones (Brinker 2010; Mills and Bone 2005; ESCOP 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000).

Digestive disturbance/dyspepsia/Help increase bile flow

Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician if symptoms persist or worsen.

Contraindication(s)

Do not use if you have a bile duct obstruction (EMA 2018; Brinker 2010; Bradley 2006; ESCOP 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000).

Known adverse reaction(s)

Stop use if hypersensitivity/allergy occurs (EMA 2018; Brinker 2010; Bradley 2006; ESCOP 2003; Blumenthal et al. 2000).

Non-medicinal ingredients

Must be chosen from the current Natural Health Products Ingredients Database (NHPID) and must meet the limitations outlined in the database.

Storage conditions

Must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural Health Products Regulations.

Specifications

  • The finished product specifications must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural and Non-prescription Health Products Directorate (NNHPD) Quality of Natural Health Products Guide.
  • The medicinal ingredient must comply with the requirements outlined in the NHPID.
