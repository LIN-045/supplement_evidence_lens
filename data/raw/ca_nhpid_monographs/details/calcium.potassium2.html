CALCIUM-POTASSIUM SALT OF HYDROXYCITRIC ACID

(PDF Version - 117 K)

This monograph is intended to serve as a guide to industry for the preparation of Product Licence Applications (PLAs) and labels for natural health product market authorization. It is not intended to be a comprehensive review of the medicinal ingredient.

Notes

  • Text in parentheses is additional optional information which can be included on the label at the applicant's discretion.
  • The solidus (/) indicates that either term and/or statement may be selected on the label.

Date

September 26, 2025

References: Proper names: Preuss et al. 2004a,b; Common names: Preuss et al. 2004a,b; Source information: Hayamizu et al. 2008; Preuss et al. 2004a,b.

Route of administration

Oral

Dosage form(s)

This monograph excludes foods or food-like dosage forms as indicated in the Compendium of Monographs Guidance Document.

Acceptable dosage forms for oral use are indicated in the dosage form drop-down list of the web-based Product Licence Application form for Compendial applications.

Use(s) or Purpose(s)

Could help temporarily increase satiety/feeling of fullness (Westerterp-Plantega and Kovacs 2002).

Note: The terms «Help’ or ‘Help to’ can be used interchangeably on the label.

Dose(s)

Subpopulation(s)

Adults 18 years and older

Quantity(ies)

1500 - 5000 milligrams, per day; Providing 500 - 2000 milligrams per single dose (Preuss et al. 2004a,b; Westerterp-Plantega and Kovacs 2002).

Potency:

  • 60% Hydroxycitric acid
  • 11% Calcium
  • 16% Potassium

Direction(s) for use

  • Take before meals (Brayfield and Cadart 2024; ASHP 2005; CPhA 2001).
  • Take a few hours before or after taking other medications or health products (Brayfield and Cadart 2024; ASHP 2005; CPhA 2001).

Duration(s) of use

Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician for use beyond 8 weeks (Preuss et al. 2004a,b).

Risk information

Caution(s) and warning(s)

  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you are breastfeeding.
  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you have a liver disorder (HC 2025).
  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician before use if you are taking medications (HC 2025).
  • Stop use and ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician if you experience any new symptoms including yellowing of the eyes or skin, dark urine, nausea, vomiting, stomach pain (HC 2025).

Contraindication(s)

All products

Do not use if you are pregnant (HC 2010).

Products providing 100 mg or more potassium, per day

Do not use if you are taking other potassium-containing salt-substitutes or supplements (Brayfield and Cadart 2024).

Products providing 350 mg or more potassium, per day or more than 200 mg potassium, per dose

Do not use if you have gastrointestinal ulceration or obstruction (Brayfield and Cadart 2024; CPhA 2001).

Known adverse reaction(s)

Products providing 350 mg or more potassium, per day or more than 200 mg potassium, per dose

Stop use and ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician if severe gastrointestinal troubles occur (Brayfield and Cadart 2024; CPhA 2001).

Non-medicinal ingredients

Must be chosen from the current Natural Health Products Ingredients Database (NHPID) and must meet the limitations outlined in the database.

Storage conditions

Must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural Health Product Regulations.

Specifications

  • The finished product specifications must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural and Non-prescription Health Products Directorate (NNHPD) Quality of Natural Health Products Guide.
  • The medicinal ingredient must comply with the requirements outlined in the NHPID.

EXAMPLE OF PRODUCT FACTS:

Consult the Guidance Document, Labelling of Natural Health Products for more details.
