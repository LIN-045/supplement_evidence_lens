CORN AND CALLUS REMOVER

(PDF Version - 49 KB)

This monograph is intended to serve as a guide to industry for the preparation of Product Licence Applications (PLAs) and labels for natural health product market authorization. It is not intended to be a comprehensive review of the medicinal ingredient.

Notes

  • Text in parentheses is additional optional information which can be included on the label at the applicant’s discretion.
  • The solidus (/) indicates that either term and/or statement may be selected on the label.
  • On the label, the exact wording of the statements from the US FDA monograph 2021, can also be used; however, the headings such as ‘When using this product’ should be kept.

Date

May 29, 2026

Proper name(s), Common name(s), Source information

Proper name(s) Common name(s) Source information
Source ingredient(s)1
2-Hydroxybenzoic acid Salicylic acid Salicylic acid

References: Proper name: RSC 2025, Gottschalck and McEwen 2004; Common name: RSC 2025, USP-NF 2024, Gottschalck and McEwen 2004; Source information: NHPID 2026

1The ingredient must be pharmacopoeial grade.

Route of administration

Topical (US FDA 2021)

Dosage form(s)

Acceptable dosage forms when used according to the requirements indicated in this monograph: Collodion-like solution; Plaster (US FDA 2021).

Use(s) or Purpose(s)

  • Corns and calluses remover (US FDA 2021).
  • (Product) for the removal of corns and calluses (US FDA 2021).
  • (Effectively) removes corns and calluses (US FDA 1990).

Additional claim1

  • Relieves pain by removing corns and calluses (US FDA 2021).

1Note: This statement can also be included, in addition to one or more of the main claims; however, it cannot be the only claim on the product label.

Optional label information

For products which contain enclosed cushions, the following additional information may be included on the product label: "(Protective) Cushion helps to relieve painful shoe pressure and friction."

Dose(s)

Subpopulation(s)

Children 2-11 years; Adolescents 12-17 years; Adults 18 years and older.

Quantity(ies)

Table 2. Doses of Salicylic acid associated with dosage forms
Dosage forms Doses
Collodion-like solution 12 – 17.6%
Plaster 12 – 40%

Reference: US FDA 2021.

Direction(s) for use

Products formulated in a plaster (US FDA 2021)

  • Wash affected area and dry thoroughly.
  • Apply (medicated) plaster and remove after 48 hours.
  • Repeat this procedure every 48 hours, as needed, (until corn/callus is removed), for up to 14 days.

Optional (if applicable)

  • Cut plaster to fit corn/callus.
  • Cover (medicated) plaster with enclosed cushion.

Products formulated in a collodion-like solution (US FDA 2021)

  • Wash affected area and dry thoroughly.
  • Apply 1 drop/a small amount at a time with applicator/brush to sufficiently cover each corn/callus. Let dry.
  • Repeat this procedure once or twice daily, as needed, (until corn/callus is removed), for up to 14 days.

Optional (if applicable)

Protective pads may be used to cushion corn/callus during treatment.

All products (Optional)

May soak corn/callus in warm water for 5 minutes to assist in removal.

Duration(s) of use

No statement required.

Risk information

Caution(s) and warning(s)

All products

  • For external use only (US FDA 2021).
  • Ask a health care practitioner/health care provider/health care professional/doctor/physician/podiatrist if discomfort persists (US FDA 2021).

Products formulated in a collodion-like solution

  • Extremely flammable/flammable/combustible (US FDA 2021).
  • Keep away from fire or flame (US FDA 2021).
  • Cap bottle tightly and store at room temperature away from heat (US FDA 2021).
  • When using this product avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water for 15 minutes (US FDA 2021).
  • When using this product avoid inhaling vapours (US FDA 2021).

Contraindication(s)

  • Do not use on irritated, infected or reddened skin (US FDA 2021).
  • Do not use if you have diabetes or poor blood circulation (US FDA 2021).

Known adverse reaction(s)

No statement required.

Non-medicinal ingredients

Must be chosen from the current Natural Health Products Ingredients Database (NHPID) and must meet the limitations outlined in the database.

Storage conditions

Must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural Health Products Regulations.

Specifications

  • The finished product specifications must be established in accordance with the requirements described in the Natural and Non-Prescription Health Products Directorate (NNHPD) Quality of Natural Health Products Guide.
  • The medicinal ingredient must comply with the requirements outlined in the NHPID.
